# Phase 13F — Complete product flow

Phase 13F turns the fork-proven architecture into a coherent personal portfolio-management experience without enabling public execution.

## Product modes and journey

Intelligence Mode begins with the existing wallet. It explains portfolio value, allocation, deterministic risk, reserve, concentration, and MARA observations while stating that nothing has been delegated. Managed Mode begins only after the user reviews the portfolio, understands the Vault boundary, selects bounded capital, creates a Financial Constitution, chooses Advisory/Approval Required/Adaptive, reviews the mandate, and reaches demo activation readiness.

The product journey is `Connect → Understand → Delegate → Constrain → Activate → Observe → Adapt → Yield → Verify`. Only explicitly delegated Vault capital may eventually be managed. Capital remaining in the wallet stays outside MARA's authority.

## Differentiation

Adaptara leads with an existing personal multi-asset portfolio, user-specific isolated Vault, personalized Constitution, market/news-aware MARA, three autonomy modes, deterministic rebalancing, and post-action verification. Aave is one bounded strategy available after portfolio and reserve needs; Adaptara is not presented as a pooled treasury or APY optimizer.

## Demo narrative

One deterministic scenario uses a $1,000 supported portfolio, 50% delegated preview, 20% liquid-reserve minimum, 50% single-asset limit, 30% aggressive limit, 10% daily adaptation, 5% action cap, 20% Aave cap, 70/30 earned-yield split, and Adaptive mode. MARA notices xETH market/news risk and recommends reducing exposure; Adaptara calculates `0.001 xETH → USD₮0`; eight Constitution checks pass; Uniswap is the fork-proven venue. A second recommendation toward 65% concentration is blocked by the 50% limit. Excess stable reserve then appears as an optional Aave opportunity, not the primary story.

## Truthful state

Vault creation, funding, mandate activation, rebalancing, and yield controls are demo/readiness presentations only. No wallet signature, public transaction, public deployment, or live autonomous authority is enabled. Uniswap and Aave are labeled X Layer fork-proven. Variable Aave supply yield is not guaranteed.
